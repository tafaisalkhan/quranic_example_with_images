Effective date: September 20, 2026

Muhammad Faisal Khan ("we", "us", or "our") provides the Quranic Examples mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information handled by the App

The App does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photographs, microphone recordings, or payment-card details.

The following preferences and activity are stored locally on your device:

  • selected application and translation languages;
  • font-size preference;
  • favorite Quranic examples;
  • local notification/reminder preferences; and
  • whether the remove-ads entitlement has been granted.

This local information is used only to provide App features. It remains on your device unless a feature described below uses a third-party service.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version displays banner and interstitial advertisements through Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • advertising ID, app-set ID, and other device or account identifiers;
  • App interactions, including launches, taps, and ad interactions; and
  • diagnostic and performance information.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Information sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID through your device's privacy and ads settings.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

3. Remove Ads purchase

The App offers a one-time Remove Ads product through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the purchase and payment information. We do not receive or store your payment-card details. The App receives purchase status, product information, and a purchase token or verification data needed to grant and restore the entitlement and prevent fraud.

Google's handling of purchase information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play Terms.

4. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App may request permission to display notifications. Reminder scheduling is performed for App functionality. You can disable notifications at any time in Android settings.

5. Sharing

When you choose to share Quranic content or an image, the App uses the Android sharing interface. You choose the destination application and recipient. The privacy practices of the receiving application or service are governed by its own policy.

6. How information is used

Information is used to provide App functionality, remember preferences, play recitations, display and measure advertisements, process or restore the remove-ads purchase, send enabled reminders, diagnose technical problems, maintain security, and prevent fraud.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed or shared with Google through AdMob, Google Play Billing, and related Google Play services for the purposes described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

8. Retention and deletion

Preferences stored by the App remain on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall it. The App does not provide user accounts, so there is no App account to delete. Google retains information handled by its services according to Google's policies and your Google account settings. You may clear local App data through Android settings.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the App. Third-party service communications use encryption in transit where provided by those services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

11. Your choices

  • Reset or delete the advertising ID in Android privacy settings.
  • Purchase Remove Ads to disable advertising within the App.
  • Clear local App information by clearing storage or uninstalling the App.
  • Manage Google activity and privacy controls through your Google account.

12. International processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their processing is governed by their privacy policies and applicable safeguards.
