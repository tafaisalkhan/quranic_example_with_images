# Privacy Policy — Quranic Examples

**Package:** `com.quranmicexmpale.daily`  
**Effective date:** September 20, 2026

Muhammad Faisal Khan ("we", "us", or "our") provides the Quranic Examples mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## Information handled by the App

The App does not require an account and does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photographs, microphone recordings, or payment-card details.

The App stores language choices, font size, favorites, reminder preferences, and the remove-ads entitlement locally on the device.

## Google AdMob

The free version uses Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or receive IP address (which can estimate general location), advertising and app-set identifiers, App/ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, ad delivery and measurement, and fraud prevention. SDK information is encrypted in transit.

Google policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Google Play Billing

The App offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase. Google Play processes payment information; we do not receive payment-card details. The App receives purchase status, product information, and purchase verification data needed to grant and restore the entitlement and prevent fraud.

## Notifications and sharing

If enabled, local reminders use Android notifications. Users can disable them in Android settings. Sharing occurs only when initiated by the user through Android's sharing interface; the selected destination service applies its own privacy policy.

## Use and sharing

Information is used to provide features, remember preferences, deliver and measure ads, process purchases, send enabled reminders, diagnose technical issues, maintain security, and prevent fraud. We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by Google through AdMob, Google Play Billing, and related Google Play services.

## Retention and deletion

Local preferences remain until the user clears App storage or uninstalls the App. The App has no user accounts. Google retains information under its own policies and user account settings.

## Security

Reasonable safeguards are used. Third-party communications use encryption in transit where provided. No electronic system is completely secure.

## Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

## User choices

Users can disable notifications, reset or delete the Android advertising ID, purchase Remove Ads, clear App storage, uninstall the App, and manage Google privacy controls.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the App, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The effective date will be revised when changes are published.
